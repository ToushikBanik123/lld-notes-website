🚀 Low-Level Design (LLD) Mastery Course

Welcome to the comprehensive Low-Level Design (LLD) course, synthesized from top industry resources including Awesome-LLD and TakeUForward.

This course is structured across 15 progressive modules, taking you from the absolute basics of Object-Oriented Design to complex, highly concurrent systems asked in senior engineering interviews.
